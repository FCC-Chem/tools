# Pre-work email

Send **7–10 days** before the workshop. Send a reminder 48 hours out to
anyone who hasn't replied.

The single biggest risk to a 90-minute session is spending 25 of those
minutes on account creation and two-factor setup. This email exists to
prevent that.

---

## Subject

**15 minutes of setup before Thursday's AI tools workshop**

## Body

Hi all,

Thursday's session is hands-on — you'll leave with a working tool that
lives at a real web address you can put in Canvas.

To make that possible in 90 minutes, I need everyone set up **before** we
start. It takes about 15 minutes. Please do it this week, not Thursday
morning.

**Three things:**

**1. Create a GitHub account** — https://github.com/signup

- Use your **work email** (`@fresnocitycollege.edu` or `@scccd.edu`). It
  unlocks free educator benefits later.
- Pick a username you'd be comfortable showing a student. Your last name
  and initial is fine.

**2. Turn on two-factor authentication** — GitHub requires it, and this is
where people get stuck if they wait until the workshop.

- Settings → Password and authentication → Enable two-factor
- Choose **authenticator app** (Google Authenticator, Microsoft
  Authenticator, or Authy) rather than text message. Text messages fail
  in our building.
- **Save the recovery codes it gives you.** Screenshot them, email them to
  yourself, write them on paper. If you lose your phone without these,
  the account is gone permanently.

**3. Reply to this email with your GitHub username.** I'll send you an
invitation to our department organization. Accept it when it arrives.

---

**Optional, but worth doing:**

Apply for free GitHub Copilot as a verified educator:
https://github.com/education/teachers

You'll need to upload something proving you teach here — a photo of your
faculty ID or a screenshot of a course schedule with your name on it.
Approval takes a few days, so start now if you want it.

---

**Bring:** a laptop you can install nothing on and still use. Everything
Thursday happens in a web browser. Mac, PC, Chromebook — all fine.

**Don't bring:** any file with student names or grades in it. We're
working in public, and we'll talk about why on Thursday.

See you then,
Rob
