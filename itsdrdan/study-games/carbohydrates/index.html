📘 How to use this game Tap to open
  1. Pick a mode. Classify Sugars, Fischer Projections, Haworth Rings, Stereochemistry, Di- & Polysaccharides or Sugar Reactions.
  2. Choose an answer and press Check Answer. Wrong choices are marked so you can try again.
  3. Need help? Hint gives a clue. Show Answer reveals the solution but earns no point.
  4. Exam Practice mixes in final-exam style questions with drawn Fischer and Haworth structures.
  5. Earn XP and badges for independent answers and streaks. Progress is saved in this browser only.

Tip: For D-sugars, an OH on the right in a Fischer projection points down in the Haworth ring.
