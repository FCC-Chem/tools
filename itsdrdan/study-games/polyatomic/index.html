Polyatomic Posse

Learn every Green Sheet ion: its name, formula and charge.

How to play
  • Pick a game below. Every question has Hint, Step by step and Show answer.
  • Type formulas the easy way: SO4^2-, SO42- or SO4-2 all become SO₄²⁻. Numbers after a letter turn into subscripts, and ^ or a + or − at the end makes the charge.
  • Element symbols are case-sensitive: Cl, not CL or cl. Names are not.
  • Decks don’t repeat an ion until you’ve seen every ion in the set.
