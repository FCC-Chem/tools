CHEM 1A

Oxidation & Reduction

Loss of Electrons = Oxidation, Gain of Electrons = Reduction. Pick a mode, answer, then press Check.

How to play
  1. Pick your course at the top, then a mode. Mixed practice shuffles every mode in your course.
  2. Type signed oxidation numbers like +2, −1 or 0. Fractions such as −8/3 work too. Enter every coefficient, including 1.
  3. Check each answer or step. Correct steps lock in; press Continue for the next step. Wrong entries are marked so you can fix just those.
  4. Hint gives a nudge. Show reveals the answer or step, but the question then earns no point.
  5. Rules (top right) opens the rules for the mode you are in. Videos are at the bottom of the page.
  6. Skill Check Prep gives 10 mixed questions from your course, then a study plan.
