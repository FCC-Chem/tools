Name That Compound!

Pick a mode, then name compounds or write their formulas.

How to play
  • Tap one or more cards below. Each card has topics you can turn on or off.
  • Formulas: just type numbers. Ca3(PO4)2 turns into Ca₃(PO₄)₂ by itself. Capital letters matter (Co is cobalt, CO is carbon monoxide).
  • Names: put Roman numerals in parentheses, like iron(III) chloride.
  • Stuck? Use Hint, Step by step or Show answer. The Rules button has every naming rule in one place.
  • Skill Check Prep gives you 10 mixed questions and a study plan.
