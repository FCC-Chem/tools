How to play
  • Tap one or more tiles above. Each tile opens its topics, which you can turn on or off.
  • Type numbers in decimal or scientific notation. 4.5E-6, 4.5e-6, 4.5×10^-6 and 4.5*10^-6 all work. You can leave the unit off.
  • Prefix symbols are case-sensitive (M is mega, m is milli). No µ key? Type u for micro.
  • Stuck? Use Hint, Step by step (it walks you through the conversion factor) or Show answer.
  • Skill Check Prep gives you 10 mixed questions, then a study plan with your weak topics.
