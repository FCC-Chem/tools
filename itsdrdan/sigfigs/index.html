Counting Significant Figures

  • All nonzero digits are significant.
  • Zeros between nonzero digits are significant.
  • Leading zeros are not significant. They only locate the decimal point.
  • Trailing zeros after a decimal point are significant.
  • For a whole number written without a decimal point, trailing zeros are normally not counted as significant in this study tool.
  • In scientific notation, count only the digits in the coefficient. The exponent does not affect the number of significant figures.
  • Exact counted quantities, such as 12 students or 6 test tubes, have an infinite number of significant figures. Enter oo.

Rounding to Significant Figures

Start counting at the first nonzero digit. Keep the requested number of significant figures, then use the next digit to decide whether to round.

Rounding to Decimal Places

Count positions to the right of the decimal point. This is different from counting significant figures.

12.347 rounded to 3 significant figures = 12.3
12.347 rounded to 3 decimal places = 12.347

Rounding Rule

If the first digit being removed is 5 or greater, increase the final retained digit by 1. If it is 4 or less, leave the final retained digit unchanged.

Scientific notation answers may be entered using E notation, such as 1.20e3.
