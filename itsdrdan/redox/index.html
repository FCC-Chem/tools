How to use this study game

  1. Choose a mode below. Start with Oxidation States or Identify REDOX, use Activity Series to predict reactivity, or practice balancing with Half-Reaction Method, Acidic REDOX, and Basic REDOX.
  2. Read the question and enter your answers. Use signed oxidation states such as +2, −1, or 0. Choose elements for oxidation and reduction, and whole reactant species for the agents. Enter every coefficient, including 1, when balancing.
  3. Check your work. Select Check Answer or Check Step. Correct steps stay locked and visible; select Continue to open the next step. Review any marked entries and try again.
  4. Get help when you need it. Hint offers guidance. References, the activity chart, and Dr. Dan’s videos can help you work through a problem. Show Answer or Show Step reveals the solution and marks the entire question as assisted, so it earns no point.
  5. Practice another problem. Finish the steps, review your completed work, then select New Question at the top. Each independently completed question earns one point; consecutive correct questions build your streak.

Study tip: Try the problem first. Pause a video to work on your own, then resume when you need the next explanation.
