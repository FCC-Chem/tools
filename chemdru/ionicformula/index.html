⚗️ Ionic Compound Builder

Drag (or tap) a cation and an anion onto the workspace. Add extra copies of each until the total positive and negative charges balance to zero — that's your neutral compound.

Type I ionic compounds

The cation is a metal that only ever forms one possible charge (most Group 1, Group 2, and a few others). No Roman numeral is needed in the name — just "metal name + anion name," e.g. calcium chloride.

Type II ionic compounds

The cation is a metal that can form more than one possible charge (many transition metals). A Roman numeral showing that specific charge is required in the name, e.g. iron (III) oxide.
