      How to Build

      Add atoms: click a ball in the Atom Bank.
      Connect atoms: click one ball, then click another to bond them.
      Add a double/triple bond: click the same two balls again.
      Remove a bond: click the connecting stick.
      Remove an atom: double-click it (or select it and press Delete).
      Rotate the view: drag anywhere on the empty workspace.
      Zoom: scroll, pinch, or use the +/− buttons.
      Click an atom, then click another to bond them.
      Tip: this workspace is a true 3D scene — rotate it to check the real shape of what you've built. (build 2026-08-09.6)
